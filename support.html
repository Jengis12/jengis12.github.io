Support

Need help with Ringlet? Check the questions below, or email cengizthepro@gmail.com. I usually reply within 2 business days.

How do I play?

Drag a ring to turn it. When every ring has a gem of the same colour on one spoke, that spoke bursts. Clear the board before you run out of moves.

I'm stuck on a level.

Try the Hint booster: it shows a good next move. Undo takes back your last move, and Restart gives you a fresh try. Every level has been checked to be solvable.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress, so it won't carry over to a new phone yet.

I bought something but didn't get it.

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Restart the game first. If it's still missing, email me with your device type and the date of the purchase. Refunds are handled through the App Store or Google Play.

How do I turn off personalised ads?

On iPhone, Ringlet asks before tracking; you can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, go to Settings > Google > Ads and delete or reset your advertising ID. In the EU and UK you can also change your choice from the privacy message in the game.

How do I ask for my data to be deleted?

Ringlet has no accounts, so I don't hold data that identifies you by name. To ask the ad and analytics partners to delete data linked to your device, email me and see the Privacy Policy for details.
