Ringlet

Spin. Match. Burst.

Coming soon to the App Store Coming soon to Google Play

Ringlet is a one-thumb puzzle game. Twist rings of glowing gems so every ring lines up the same colour along a spoke, and watch the whole spoke burst. Plan ahead: one twist can set up a combo, and the wrong one can leave a gem stranded.

200 handcrafted levels

Every level is checked by a solver, so every puzzle can be beaten.

New twists every world

Stones, locked rings, geared rings, bombs, hidden gems and rainbow wildcards.

A new daily puzzle

One fresh puzzle every day, the same for every player.

Play your way

Short levels, no timers (except the bombs!), and colour-blind friendly gem shapes.
