Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy explains how Ringlet (the "game"), made by Cengiz Saydam ("I", "me"), handles information when you play. In short: Ringlet has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name or email. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads and uses analytics to stay free, and those services collect some device information, described below.

1. Information stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, coins, boosters and settings (such as sound) are saved only on your device. I can't see them, and they are deleted if you delete the game.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The game uses the services below. They may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only with your permission; Google Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device model, operating system, language, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads and the game.

I use analytics in aggregate (for example, how many players finish level 10) to improve the game. I don't sell your personal information.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. I never receive your payment details; I only receive confirmation that a purchase happened so the game can deliver it.

4. Your choices

  • iPhone: Ringlet asks for permission before using your advertising identifier. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads.
  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the game's privacy settings.
  • Ads in general: if you decline personalised ads, you'll still see ads, but they won't be based on your activity. The "Remove Ads" purchase removes non-rewarded ads.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information, and to object to or limit how it's used. Because Ringlet has no accounts, I don't hold information that identifies you by name. Most data is held by the services above, and you can contact them directly using the links in section 2. You can also email me and I'll help pass on your request.

6. Children

Ringlet is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, please contact me so I can help remove it.

7. How long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete the game. Third-party services keep data according to their own policies.

8. Security

The game and the services above send data over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but I choose established providers and keep the data the game uses to a minimum.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the effective date at the top.
